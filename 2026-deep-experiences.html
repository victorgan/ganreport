Every entry here shares three properties: stakes you can't exit cheaply, reciprocity you don't control, and time you can't compress. Modern life is engineered to remove all three, which is why a borrowed cat can feel like a revelation. Depth below is not difficulty — it's how much of you the thing takes, and how hard it is to come back up.
