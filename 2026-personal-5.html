Goal catalogue

A menu, not a checklist. Thresholds for domains currently below the line are in scope now. Four to six ambitious goals across five years. Exactly three lofty ones, across uncorrelated failure modes. Adopting a goal creates its anticipatory target and a binding stub you then have to quantify.

The plan

Ceilings: eight active binding targets, three lofty goals, four domains at major emphasis or above. These are limits, not objectives.
The rule that does the work: never set a binding target on another person's behaviour. Friendship, love, recognition and audience are all consumption-share problems — they matter most, cannot be commanded, and the only honest move is to command the exposure and initiation that make them likely.

Gating decisions

A question without a date isn't open, it's avoided. Any decision gating three or more domains is forced in Year 1 — the carrying cost of deferral compounds; the cost of choosing wrong is bounded.

Reviews

Ninety minutes, four times a year, hard stop. A missed target cannot be left unclassified. Retire at least one target at every review — a plan that only grows gets abandoned whole rather than pruned.
A — delete the target, one sentence on why it looked right. B — recompute the input rate. Raise it or extend the deadline. Not both. C — log the assumption that broke, then reset. D — change nothing about the target. Reduce the number of active binding targets instead.

Infrastructure cap: four hours per quarter on the plan's own machinery, including this page. Exceeding it is a Class D failure against the plan itself.

Deliberately absent: streaks, notifications, a composite life score, anything rewarding you for opening the app. Four times a year is correct. Export keeps the JSON beside the spec.
